About Alpha-Genesis
Construction Corp.

A lifetime of stair work, passed down through generations.

Alpha-Genesis Construction Corp. is owned and operated by Levi Prest.

We were founded by my father Richard Prest in 2000.

I have spent my life perfecting my skills in this trade, learning from my dad firsthand—not only how to build stairs, but to take pride in the details, respect the work, and do things the right way.

Everything I know about finish stair work comes from over 20 years of hands-on experience, jobsite lessons, and carrying on the high standards this company was built on.

Today, I’m proud to lead Alpha-Genesis Construction Corp. into its next chapter while keeping that same family-first heritage at the center of every project.

My focus is simple: extremely high-quality finish stair work, clean craftsmanship, and results that homeowners can be proud of.

From custom hardwood staircases to handrails, posts, balusters, and cable rail systems, every job gets my personal attention and commitment to excellence.

Our quality guarantee

Work that looks beautiful, feels sturdy, and lasts.

Alpha-Genesis Construction Corp. takes pride in stair work that is clean, solid, and built with long-term quality in mind. Every staircase, handrail, post, baluster, and cable system is installed with careful attention to fit, finish, alignment, and detail.

We believe high-end stair work should match the character of the home. From custom hardwood staircases to production stair systems, our goal is to deliver dependable craftsmanship without cutting corners.

As an owner-operated, family-rooted company serving San Diego County since 2000, we stand behind the experience, stair knowledge, and pride that go into every project.
